🎓 UNDERSTANDING RESULTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  RMSE = Root Mean Square Error
  • Lower is better
  • Example: RMSE=5.2 means typical error ±5.2 units

  MAE = Mean Absolute Error
  • Average absolute error
  • Example: MAE=3.8 means typical error 3.8 units

  R² = Coefficient of Determination
  • 0-1 scale (higher is better)
  • R²=0.9  means model explains 90% of variance (EXCELLENT)
  • R²=0.8  means 80% variance explained (GOOD)
  • R²<0.6  means <60% explained (QUESTIONABLE)
